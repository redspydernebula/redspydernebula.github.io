Bio:

Hi! My name is Andrew Boyacigiller. I am currently attending Brown University where I am studying Computer Science and Music. I have ran through a gamut of projects and disciplines in my time here, focusing primarily on computer vision, graphics, and computer security. I am particularly interested in the intersection of art and technology. In this vein, you can find my work in p5js from my earlier years, but I have also worked with interactive sculpture, I have found myself particularly enjoying seminar style courses and those where the projects center around implementing a paper or series of papers. In terms of music, I have been playing piano on and off for over a decade and have been composing for about 5 years. I have an interest in electronic music, and I have been using Ableton Live to produce music for the past 7 years. Currently, I am studying vocal performance under Tyler Cesario, under which I am currently studying French impressionist music and classic opera repertoire. I am performing and regularly soloing in the as a Tenor Voice Scholar at Trinity Church, as well as performing in Providence and the wider New England area. Originally from just outside of Annapolis MD, I has moved and performed all over the U.S. from Texas to Georgia, and now Rhode Island. Outside of academic work, I enjoy composing, coding interactive art using p5.js, and drafting technical drawings. In my free time, I enjoy sailing with my dad, reading or watching old-school science fiction, and attempting to cook passable meals with an increasingly dwindling number of ingredients. I also have an interest in rocketry, relativistic light transport, vocal acoustics, and the mathematics found in just intonation.
